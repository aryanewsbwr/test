1. DISCOUNT PRIORITY & CONFLICT RESOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sources of Discount in Database:
  A. Subscription-level discount: cd.dis (or cd.discount_percent) in Customer_Detail
  B. Customer-level discount:     cust.discount (or cust.dis) in Customer Master

PRIORITY RULE:
  Subscription-level discount (cd.dis) STRICTLY OVERRIDES Customer-level discount (cust.discount).
  They NEVER stack, combine, or compound.

Formula for Line Item Discount:
  If (cd.dis is set and cd.dis > 0):
      Effective_Discount_Percent = cd.dis
  Else if (cust.discount is set and cust.discount > 0):
      Effective_Discount_Percent = cust.discount
  Else:
      Effective_Discount_Percent = 0

  Line_Discount_Amount = Line_Paper_Total * (Effective_Discount_Percent / 100)

Total Bill Discount (Dis_Amt in BillNo):
  Total_Discount_Amount = Sum of all Line_Discount_Amount for that customer

CRITICAL CONSTRAINTS:
  - Discounts apply ONLY to the Paper Total (newspaper/magazine charges).
  - Discounts NEVER apply to Delivery Charges (cd.dely / Del_Amt).
  - Discounts NEVER apply to Previous Dues (Due_Amt) or Retail Counter Sales.
  - Example: If a customer has cust.discount = 10%, but their Times of India subscription
    has cd.dis = 15%, the discount applied on Times of India is 15% (NOT 25%, NOT 23.5%).

2. PERIODICALS / MAGAZINES DISCONTINUATION & HOLIDAY RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. DISCONTINUATION (Permanent 'P' and Temporary 'T' Suspensions):
   YES - Discontinuation rules strictly apply to ALL periodicals (Weekly, Fortnightly,
   Monthly, Quarterly) just like daily newspapers.

   - Weekly Magazines:
     Checked on each occurrence of the magazine's designated publication weekday (MagzineDay).
     If the customer has an active discontinuation (P or T) on that date, the issue is
     skipped and NOT billed.

   - Fortnightly & Bi-Monthly Magazines (Published on 1st and 16th of each month):
     Evaluated separately on the 1st and 16th of the month.
     Example: If a customer is under temporary suspension ('T') from 10th to 20th of the month:
       - 1st issue: DELIVERED and BILLED.
       - 16th issue: SKIPPED and NOT BILLED.
     Customer is never overbilled.

   - Monthly & Quarterly Magazines (Published on 1st of each month):
     Evaluated on the 1st of the month.
     If customer has an active discontinuation on the 1st, the issue is skipped and NOT billed.

B. HOLIDAYS (Press Holidays):
   - Daily Newspapers:
     Skip delivery on official press holidays (Holi, Diwali, Independence Day, etc.)
     recorded in the Holiday table. Customers are not billed for these days.
   - Periodicals / Magazines:
     Do NOT skip on general daily press holidays.
     Magazines are pre-printed and delivered on their release schedule. A daily newspaper
     press holiday does not cancel magazine issues.

3. EXACT MATHEMATICAL BILLING FORMULAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FORMULA 1: DAILY NEWSPAPERS (दैनिक समाचार पत्र)
-----------------------------------------------
For each calendar day (d = 1 to Total_Days_In_Month):

  Delivery Condition (Day is delivered IF AND ONLY IF):
    1. Target_Date >= cd.s_date  (Subscription start date)
    AND
    2. (cd.c_date is empty OR Target_Date < cd.c_date)  (Subscription close date)
    AND
    3. Target_Date is NOT in Holidays for this publication
    AND
    4. Target_Date is NOT in Discontinuation range (P or T) for this customer & publication
    AND
    5. DayOfWeek(Target_Date) matches cd.from_day delivery schedule (e.g. 1-7 for Daily)

  Rate Determination on Day d:
    If RateChange entry exists for this publication with Dated <= Target_Date:
        Rate(d) = Latest NewRate from RateChange
    Else:
        Rate(d) = Standard Rate for this DayOfWeek from Rate table

  Line_Paper_Total = Sum(Delivered_Days at Rate_R) * Rate_R * cd.qty


FORMULA 2: WEEKLY MAGAZINES (साप्ताहिक पत्रिका)
-----------------------------------------------
Delivered on the publication's designated weekday (pub.magzine_day: 1=Sun, 2=Mon... 7=Sat).

  Delivered_Copies = Count of days in month where:
    DayOfWeek(d) == pub.magzine_day
    AND Target_Date >= cd.s_date
    AND (cd.c_date is empty OR Target_Date < cd.c_date)
    AND Target_Date is NOT in Discontinuation range (P or T)

  Weekly_Paper_Total = Delivered_Copies * Effective_Rate * cd.qty


FORMULA 3: FORTNIGHTLY & BI-MONTHLY PERIODICALS (पाक्षिक पत्रिका)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Delivered on the 1st and 16th of the billing month:

  Delivered_Copies = Count of dates in {1st, 16th} where:
    Date <= Total_Days_In_Month
    AND Date >= cd.s_date
    AND (cd.c_date is empty OR Date < cd.c_date)
    AND Date is NOT in Discontinuation range (P or T)

  Fortnightly_Paper_Total = Delivered_Copies * Effective_Rate * cd.qty


FORMULA 4: MONTHLY & QUARTERLY MAGAZINES (मासिक एवं त्रैमासिक)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Delivered on the 1st of the billing month:

  Eligibility Check:
    - Monthly Magazines: Delivered every month.
    - Quarterly Magazines (e.g. pub_id = 75): Delivered only in months 1 (Jan), 4 (Apr), 7 (Jul), 10 (Oct).

  Delivered_Copies = 1 IF:
    Eligible for this month
    AND Month_Start >= cd.s_date
    AND (cd.c_date is empty OR Month_Start < cd.c_date)
    AND 1st of month is NOT in Discontinuation range (P or T)
  ELSE:
    Delivered_Copies = 0

  Monthly_Paper_Total = Delivered_Copies * Effective_Rate * cd.qty


FORMULA 5: DELIVERY CHARGES (वितरण शुल्क)
-----------------------------------------
Delivery charge is defined per subscription line item (cd.dely / cd.delivery_charge):

  Total_Delivery_Charge = Sum of cd.dely for all active subscriptions of the customer
  (Active = subscription was alive during the billing month)


FORMULA 6: TOTAL DISCOUNT (छूट)
-------------------------------
Calculated line-by-line across all subscriptions of the customer:

  Total_Discount = Sum of [ Subscription_Paper_Total * (Effective_Discount_Percent / 100) ]
  (Stored in BillNo.Dis_Amt)


FORMULA 7: PREVIOUS DUE (पूर्व बकाया राशि - LEDGER BRING-FORWARD)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Calculated from the beginning of historical records up to prior month:

  Opening_Due     = cust.dueamount  (Customer opening balance)
  Total_Billed    = Sum of (Prior Months' Paper_Total + Prior Months' Del_Amt)
  Total_Receipts  = Sum of (All Receipts paid by customer: MalRecpAmt + LessAmt)

  Previous_Due    = Opening_Due + Total_Billed - Total_Receipts
  (Stored in BillNo.Due_Amt)


FORMULA 8: GRAND TOTAL / NET BALANCE PAYABLE (कुल देय राशि)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Total amount payable by the customer for the bill:

  Grand_Total (BillNo.Balance) = Previous_Due
                                + Total_Paper_Amount
                                + Total_Delivery_Charge
                                - Total_Discount

4. DATABASE SCHEMA MAPPING REFERENCE
================================================================================

Table: billYYYYYYYY (Line items breakdown)
  - Bill_id     : Unique bill number
  - Customer_id : Customer ID
  - Publica_id  : Publication ID
  - Region_id   : Customer's Region ID
  - Qty         : Total copies delivered in month (Days * Qty)
  - Rate        : Effective rate per copy
  - D_Charges   : Delivery charges (null or amount)
  - TotalAmt    : Qty * Rate (Paper amount for this line)
  - Month       : Month name (e.g. 'April', 'December')
  - year        : Start year of financial year (e.g. '2025')
  - sno         : Serial number of line item in bill

Table: billnoYYYYYYYY (Bill summary header)
  - Bill_id     : Unique bill number
  - Customer_id : Customer ID
  - Region_id   : Customer's Region ID
  - Due_Amt     : Previous due brought forward (or null if 0)
  - Del_Amt     : Total delivery charges for month (or null if 0)
  - Dis_Amt     : Total discount given for month (or null if 0)
  - Month       : Month name
  - year        : Start year of financial year
  - Balance     : Final net payable amount (Due_Amt + PaperTotal + Del_Amt - Dis_Amt)
