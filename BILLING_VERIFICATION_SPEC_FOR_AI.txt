1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURAL INVARIANTS
================================================================================
- Single Source of Truth: Invoices and monthly bills are derived from transactional
  event records (deliveries, holidays, suspensions, rate matrices, and receipts).
- Zero Stacking: Subscription-specific overrides strictly supersede customer-level defaults.
- Financial Year Boundary Awareness: The accounting year follows the Indian fiscal
  calendar (April 1st to March 31st). Billing tables are partitioned annually
  (`billnoYYYYYYYY`, `billYYYYYYYY`, `receiptYYYYYYYY`).
- Stored/Cached Balance Immunity: `Customer.dueamount` is recognized as a static 2007
  baseline and is NEVER used as the active opening balance in ongoing financial years.

2. DISCOUNT PRIORITY & STACKING CONFLICT RESOLUTION
================================================================================
A. DATABASE COLUMNS INVOLVED:
   1. Subscription-level: `cd.dis` (or `cd.discount_percent`) in `Customer_Detail`
   2. Customer-level:     `cust.discount` (or `cust.dis`) in `Customer` master
   3. Bill summary:       `billnoYYYYYYYY.Dis_Amt`

B. RESOLUTION HIERARCHY (NO STACKING / NO COMPOUNDING):
   For each individual subscription line item i:

   Case 1: Subscription has explicit discount (cd.dis is NOT NULL and cd.dis > 0)
     Effective_Discount_Percent = cd.dis
     (Subscription discount strictly overrides any customer default)

   Case 2: Subscription discount is 0 or NULL, but customer has global discount
     Effective_Discount_Percent = cust.discount

   Case 3: Neither is set (> 0)
     Effective_Discount_Percent = 0

   MATHEMATICAL RULE:
     Effective_Dis_Percent = (cd.dis > 0) ? cd.dis : ((cust.discount > 0) ? cust.discount : 0)

   Line_Discount_Amount = Line_Paper_Total * (Effective_Dis_Percent / 100)

C. CONSTRAINTS:
   - Subscription discount and Customer discount NEVER stack (e.g., 10% global + 15% sub
     yields 15%, NOT 25%, and NOT 23.5%).
   - Discount applies EXCLUSIVELY to Line_Paper_Total (newspaper/magazine charges).
   - Discount NEVER applies to Delivery Charges (cd.dely / Del_Amt).
   - Discount NEVER applies to Previous Dues (Due_Amt) or Retail Counter Sales.
   - Total Monthly Discount:
       Dis_Amt = Sum of all Line_Discount_Amount for that customer

3. PERIODICALS / MAGAZINES DISCONTINUATION & SUSPENSION SCOPE
================================================================================
A. DISCONTINUATION (Permanent 'P' and Temporary 'T'):
   Strict Rule: Discontinuation rules APPLY TO ALL PERIODICALS (Weekly, Fortnightly,
   Monthly, Quarterly) with identical `publica_id in (0, cd.publica_id)` matching as daily newspapers.

   1. Weekly Magazines (e.g., India Today, Panchjanya):
      - Evaluated on each occurrence of the magazine's designated publication weekday
        (`pub.magzine_day`: 1=Sun, 2=Mon ... 7=Sat).
      - Condition:
        NOT (Discontinue.customer_id == cd.customer_id
             AND Discontinue.publica_id in (0, cd.publica_id)
             AND Target_Date in [Temp_From .. Temp_To / Forever])
      - If customer has an active suspension on that date: Issue is SKIPPED and NOT BILLED.

   2. Fortnightly & Bi-Monthly Magazines (e.g., Sarita, Champak - 1st & 16th):
      - Evaluated INDEPENDENTLY on the 1st and the 16th of the billing month.
      - Condition on Date d (d in {1, 16}):
        NOT (Discontinue.customer_id == cd.customer_id
             AND Discontinue.publica_id in (0, cd.publica_id)
             AND Date in [Temp_From .. Temp_To / Forever])
      - Example: Customer is on temporary vacation suspension ('T') from 10th to 20th:
        * 1st of month: Active -> DELIVERED and BILLED.
        * 16th of month: Falls inside [10th..20th] -> SKIPPED and NOT BILLED.
      - Result: Customer is billed for exactly 1 copy instead of 2.

   3. Monthly & Quarterly Magazines (Delivered on 1st of month):
      - Evaluated on the 1st of the month:
        NOT (Discontinue.customer_id == cd.customer_id
             AND Discontinue.publica_id in (0, cd.publica_id)
             AND 1st_of_Month in [Temp_From .. Temp_To / Forever])
      - If customer is discontinued on the 1st: Issue is SKIPPED and NOT BILLED.

4. HOLIDAY SCOPE & GENERAL PRESS HOLIDAY IMPLEMENTATION
================================================================================
A. DATABASE SCHEMA FOR HOLIDAYS:
   Table: `Holiday`
   Columns:
     - `holiday_id` : Primary Key
     - `oc_date`    : Occasion Date (DD/MM/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD)
     - `occasion`   : Text name (e.g. '15august', 'Diwali', 'Holi')
     - `publica_id` : Publication ID (Integer, or NULL / 0)

B. GENERAL PRESS HOLIDAYS vs PUBLICATION-SPECIFIC HOLIDAYS:
   1. `publica_id = 0` OR `publica_id IS NULL`:
      GENERAL PRESS HOLIDAY. Represents nationwide press closure (e.g., Diwali, Holi).
      Applies to ALL daily newspapers.
   2. `publica_id > 0`:
      PUBLICATION-SPECIFIC HOLIDAY. Applies ONLY to that particular publication.

C. MATCHING LOGIC IN CODE:
   - For Daily Newspapers (Formula 1, Condition 3):
     A calendar day `Target_Date` is a HOLIDAY IF AND ONLY IF:
       Holiday.oc_date == Target_Date
       AND (Holiday.publica_id == 0 OR Holiday.publica_id IS NULL OR Holiday.publica_id == cd.publica_id)

     -> In TypeScript (`src/lib/billingEngine.ts`):
        `return hIso === targetDateIso && (!hPub || hPub === 0 || hPub === publicaId);`
     -> Result: General holidays (publica_id = 0/NULL) match EVERY daily newspaper.
        No customer is billed for daily papers on days the printing press is closed.

   - For Periodicals / Magazines:
     Magazines are pre-printed and delivered on distribution cycles. They do NOT skip
     on general daily press holidays (`publica_id = 0`). They ONLY skip if a holiday
     record specifically names that exact magazine ID (`Holiday.publica_id == pubId`).

5. FORMULA 7: PREVIOUS DUE, OPENING BALANCE & FINANCIAL YEAR (FY) BOUNDARY
================================================================================
A. DIRECT ANSWER TO THE ARCHITECTURAL AUDIT QUESTION:
   Question: Is Opening_Due computed live from bill/receipt tables, or read from a stored dueamount column?
   Answer:
     - Opening_Due is NEVER read from `Customer.dueamount` in ongoing financial years!
     - `Customer.dueamount` is a STATIC 2007 baseline field created when the customer
       was first entered into the software 18 years ago. It NEVER updates.
     - Reading `Customer.dueamount` in subsequent financial years was the EXACT bug that
       caused the historical ₹864,447 discrepancy.

B. HOW THE FINANCIAL YEAR (FY) ANCHOR OPERATES:
   Aryan News Agency partitions billing data annually:
     `billnoYYYYYYYY`  (e.g., `billno20252026` for FY 2025-2026)
     `billYYYYYYYY`    (e.g., `bill20252026`)
     `receiptYYYYYYYY` (e.g., `receipt20252026`)

   On April 1st of each Financial Year, the year-end changeover process calculates each
   customer's audited net closing balance as of March 31st and writes it into the new FY's
   table as an anchor row:
     Table: `billnoYYYYYYYY`
     Row criteria: `Month = 'Dues'`
     Stored value: `Due_Amt` = March 31st Closing Balance (can be positive for dues, negative for advance)

   Concrete Proof from Database:
     Customer #108:
       `Customer.dueamount` in customer master: 60  (frozen from 2007)
       `billno20252026` where Month='Dues': Due_Amt = 2723 (actual audited carry-forward)
     Customer #1384:
       `Customer.dueamount` in customer master: 399 (frozen from 2007)
       `billno20252026` where Month='Dues': Due_Amt = -115 (advance payment carry-forward)

C. HANDLING THE "8 MISSING ROLLOVER CUSTOMERS" EDGE CASE:
   During the legacy VB6 annual rollover batch, customers with zero active paper subscriptions
   in March were sometimes skipped, omitting their `Month = 'Dues'` row in the new table.
   The replacement billing engine handles this gracefully:
     If customer HAS a `Month = 'Dues'` row in current FY table:
         Year_End_Dues = billnoYYYYYYYY.Due_Amt (where Month = 'Dues')
     Else If customer exists in Prior FY table (`billno(Prior_FY)`):
         Year_End_Dues = billno(Prior_FY).Balance [March] (live lookup from previous year)
     Else:
         Year_End_Dues = Customer.dueamount (only for brand-new customers created in current FY)

   CRITICAL RULE:
   `Year_End_Dues` and `Customer.dueamount` are NEVER summed together.

D. WITHIN-FY LIVE CALCULATION FOR BILLING MONTH M:
   When generating bills for month M (e.g., August, which is FY month index 4):
   Financial Year Month Ordering:
     [April=0, May=1, June=2, July=3, August=4, September=5, October=6, November=7, December=8, January=9, February=10, March=11]

   1. Starting FY Balance:
      Year_End_Dues = billnoYYYYYYYY.Due_Amt (where Month = 'Dues')

   2. Prior Billed Charges in Current FY:
      Sum all bills for months m < M in the current FY table (`billnoYYYYYYYY` / `billYYYYYYYY`):
        Prior_Months = { April, May, June, July }
        Prior_Billed_In_FY = Sum of [ Month_Paper_Total + Month_Del_Amt - Month_Dis_Amt ] for m in Prior_Months

   3. Prior Receipts Collected in Current FY:
      Sum all customer collections from `receiptYYYYYYYY` recorded before the 1st of Month M:
        Prior_Receipts_In_FY = Sum of [ MalRecpAmt + LessAmt ]

   4. Final Previous Due Formula for Month M:
      Previous_Due(Month M) = Year_End_Dues + Prior_Billed_In_FY - Prior_Receipts_In_FY

   This guarantees 100% mathematical precision, spans the FY boundary safely, and prevents
   any drift from stored balance columns.

6. COMPLETE MATHEMATICAL BILLING FORMULAS
================================================================================

FORMULA 1: DAILY NEWSPAPERS (दैनिक समाचार पत्र)
-----------------------------------------------
For each day d from 1 to Total_Days_In_Month:
  Deliver Day d IF AND ONLY IF:
    1. Target_Date >= cd.s_date
    AND 2. (cd.c_date is empty OR Target_Date < cd.c_date)
    AND 3. Target_Date is NOT in Holidays:
           NOT (Holiday.oc_date == Target_Date AND (Holiday.publica_id == 0 OR Holiday.publica_id IS NULL OR Holiday.publica_id == cd.publica_id))
    AND 4. Target_Date is NOT in Discontinue:
           NOT (Discontinue.customer_id == cd.customer_id AND Discontinue.publica_id in (0, cd.publica_id) AND Target_Date in [Temp_From .. Temp_To/Forever])
    AND 5. DayOfWeek(Target_Date) in Schedule(cd.from_day)

  Rate on Day d (DayOfWeek: 1=Sun .. 7=Sat):
    RateChange Resolution:
      Filter matching RateChange rows:
        RateChange.Publica_id == cd.publica_id
        AND RateChange.Dayofweek in (0, NULL, DayOfWeek(Target_Date))
        AND RateChange.Dated <= Target_Date
      Sort Priority:
        1. Dated DESC (most recent rate change wins)
        2. Exact DayOfWeek match (1-7) wins over general DayOfWeek (0) on same date
    Fallback:
      If no RateChange matches, use standard Rate for this DayOfWeek from Rate table (Publica_id, Dayofweek).

  Daily_Paper_Total = Sum(Delivered_Days_at_Rate_R) * Rate_R * cd.qty


FORMULA 2: WEEKLY MAGAZINES (साप्ताहिक पत्रिका)
-----------------------------------------------
Delivered on publication's designated weekday (`pub.magzine_day`: 1=Sun, 2=Mon... 7=Sat).
  Delivered_Copies = Count of calendar dates d in month where:
    DayOfWeek(d) == pub.magzine_day
    AND Target_Date >= cd.s_date
    AND (cd.c_date is empty OR Target_Date < cd.c_date)
    AND Target_Date is NOT in Discontinuation range:
        NOT (Discontinue.customer_id == cd.customer_id AND Discontinue.publica_id in (0, cd.publica_id) AND Target_Date in [Temp_From .. Temp_To/Forever])
    AND Target_Date is NOT in Magazine-Specific Holiday (Holiday.publica_id == pub.publica_id)

  Weekly_Paper_Total = Delivered_Copies * Effective_Rate * cd.qty


FORMULA 3: FORTNIGHTLY & BI-MONTHLY PERIODICALS (पाक्षिक पत्रिका)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Delivered on 1st and 16th of the billing month.
  Delivered_Copies = Count of dates in {1st, 16th} where:
    Date <= Total_Days_In_Month
    AND Date >= cd.s_date
    AND (cd.c_date is empty OR Date < cd.c_date)
    AND Date is NOT in Discontinuation range:
        NOT (Discontinue.customer_id == cd.customer_id AND Discontinue.publica_id in (0, cd.publica_id) AND Date in [Temp_From .. Temp_To/Forever])

  Fortnightly_Paper_Total = Delivered_Copies * Effective_Rate * cd.qty


FORMULA 4: MONTHLY & QUARTERLY MAGAZINES (मासिक एवं त्रैमासिक)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Delivered on 1st of the billing month.
  Eligibility:
    - Monthly Magazines: Delivered in all 12 months.
    - Quarterly Magazines (e.g., pub_id = 75): Delivered ONLY in Month in {1 (Jan), 4 (Apr), 7 (Jul), 10 (Oct)}.

  Delivered_Copies = 1 IF:
    Eligible for Month
    AND Month_Start >= cd.s_date
    AND (cd.c_date is empty OR Month_Start < cd.c_date)
    AND 1st of month is NOT in Discontinuation range:
        NOT (Discontinue.customer_id == cd.customer_id AND Discontinue.publica_id in (0, cd.publica_id) AND Month_Start in [Temp_From .. Temp_To/Forever])
  ELSE:
    Delivered_Copies = 0

  Monthly_Paper_Total = Delivered_Copies * Effective_Rate * cd.qty


FORMULA 5: DELIVERY CHARGES (वितरण शुल्क)
-----------------------------------------
Defined per subscription line item:
  Total_Delivery_Charge = Sum of cd.dely for all active subscriptions in billing month


FORMULA 6: LINE & TOTAL DISCOUNT (छूट)
--------------------------------------
  Effective_Discount_Percent = (cd.dis > 0) ? cd.dis : ((cust.discount > 0) ? cust.discount : 0)
  Line_Discount = Line_Paper_Total * (Effective_Discount_Percent / 100)
  Total_Discount = Sum of Line_Discount for all customer subscriptions (stored in BillNo.Dis_Amt)


FORMULA 7: PREVIOUS DUE (पूर्व बकाया)
-------------------------------------
  Previous_Due(Month M) = Year_End_Dues (Month='Dues')
                          + Sum of Prior Month Billed in FY
                          - Sum of Prior Receipts in FY
  (Stored in BillNo.Due_Amt)


FORMULA 8: GRAND TOTAL NET PAYABLE (कुल देय राशि)
-------------------------------------------------
  Grand_Total (BillNo.Balance) = Previous_Due
                                + Total_Paper_Amount
                                + Total_Delivery_Charge
                                - Total_Discount

8. DATABASE SCHEMA CROSS-REFERENCE
================================================================================

1. `billYYYYYYYY` (Line item breakdown table):
   - Bill_id     : Unique bill number
   - Customer_id : Customer foreign key
   - Publica_id  : Publication foreign key
   - Region_id   : Distribution route/region
   - Qty         : Total copies delivered in month (Days * Qty)
   - Rate        : Effective rate per copy
   - D_Charges   : Delivery charges (if line-specific, or null)
   - TotalAmt    : Line paper amount (Qty * Rate)
   - Month       : Month name string ('April' ... 'March')
   - year        : Financial year start string ('2025')
   - sno         : Serial number of item in bill

2. `billnoYYYYYYYY` (Monthly bill summary header):
   - Bill_id     : Unique bill number
   - Customer_id : Customer foreign key
   - Region_id   : Region ID
   - Due_Amt     : Previous Due brought forward (Month='Dues' stores FY rollover)
   - Del_Amt     : Total monthly delivery charges
   - Dis_Amt     : Total monthly discount
   - Month       : Month name string ('Dues', 'April' ... 'March')
   - year        : Financial year start string ('2025')
   - Balance     : Net balance payable (Due_Amt + PaperTotal + Del_Amt - Dis_Amt)

3. `receiptYYYYYYYY` (Cash/Cheque receipts):
   - receipt_id   : Unique receipt ID
   - bill_id      : Associated Bill ID
   - receipt_no   : Receipt voucher number
   - mal_recp_dt  : Collection payment date
   - month        : Month for which payment was applied
   - mal_recp_amt : Cash/Cheque amount paid
   - less_amt     : Allowed deduction/concession
   - customer_id  : Customer foreign key
